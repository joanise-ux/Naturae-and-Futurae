// who we are

A laboratory in the heart of the Owl Mountains

Nature & Future is a plant micropropagation laboratory based in Wałbrzych. We combine the precision of in vitro technology with a passion for saving historic varieties — sour cherries, sweet cherries and apple trees from orchards that have grown here for over a hundred years.

We also produce aquarium plants in in vitro culture — 100% free of algae, snails and pathogens.

// our story

The orchard in Walim — over a hundred years under the Owl Mountains sky

„Old trees survived two wars and half a century of neglect. We carry their genes into the future — test tube by test tube."

The Owl Mountains region, the estates of the von Zedlitz and von Seher-Thoss families, the palace in Jedlinka. From 18th-century orchards, through the wars and the communist era, to our in vitro laboratory.
